Our Story

Built for Measurable Growth, Not Vanity Metrics

RankLinkus was co-founded in 2013 by Shakeel Akram and Ali Azaan with a single belief: that businesses deserve a digital marketing partner that actually delivers what it promises. No inflated metrics, no black-hat shortcuts, no smoke and mirrors.

Over 12 years, we've grown from a small outreach team into a full-service SEO agency with offices in Pakistan, the UK, the US, and Estonia — serving 900+ international clients across every industry imaginable.

Our edge? We treat every client like a partner, not an invoice number. Your goals become our goals. Your wins are our wins. And when something isn't working, we tell you — then fix it.
