Pricing Questions Answered

Are there any setup fees or contracts?

+
No setup fees and no long-term contracts. All plans are month-to-month and can be cancelled with 30 days notice. We earn your business every month.

How quickly will I see results?

+
Link building typically shows ranking improvements within 6–12 weeks. Digital PR can generate immediate traffic. SEO is a long-term investment — most clients see significant traffic growth within 3–6 months.

Do you offer white-label services for agencies?

+
Yes. We power SEO and link building for 200+ agencies under their own brand. All deliverables are fully unbranded and we sign NDAs. Contact us for white-label pricing.

What does "DR70+" mean?

+
DR stands for Domain Rating, an Ahrefs metric measuring a website's backlink authority on a scale of 0–100. DR70+ sites are high-authority publications — think established news sites, industry blogs, and major online magazines. These links carry significant SEO weight.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan?

+
Absolutely. You can change plans at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades take effect from the next billing cycle.

How do I track progress?

+
Every client gets access to a live reporting dashboard showing all published links, keyword rankings, traffic data, and DA metrics. You also receive a detailed monthly report from your account manager.
