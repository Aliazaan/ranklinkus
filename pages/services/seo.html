FAQ

Common Questions

What does a technical SEO audit cover?

+
Our audit covers 87 checks including Core Web Vitals, crawl budget, XML sitemaps, hreflang, canonical tags, structured data, page speed, mobile usability, and internal linking architecture.

How long does SEO take to show results?

+
Most clients see measurable traffic improvements within 3–4 months. Competitive niches may take 6–9 months for significant movement. SEO is a long-term investment that compounds over time.

Do you do local SEO?

+
Yes. We build NAP citations, optimise Google Business Profiles, target local keywords, and build location-specific landing pages for businesses targeting local customers.

Can you recover a site from a Google penalty?

+
Yes. We have extensive experience recovering sites from manual actions and algorithmic updates. We conduct a full audit, remove or disavow toxic links, and restore quality content to recover rankings.
