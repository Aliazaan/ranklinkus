FAQ

Link Building Questions

Do you use PBNs or link farms?

+
Never. Every link we build is a genuine editorial placement on a real website with real traffic. We have never used PBNs, link farms, or any black-hat technique in our 12 years of operation.

How long until I see ranking improvements?

+
Most clients see measurable ranking movement within 6–12 weeks. Competitive niches may take 3–4 months for significant movement. We provide monthly rank tracking so you can see progress clearly.

Can I approve target sites before you outreach?

+
Yes. We can share a pre-approved prospecting list for your review before any outreach begins — available on Growth and Authority plans.

What if a link disappears after placement?

+
We offer a 90-day replacement guarantee on all placements. If a link is removed within 90 days of delivery, we replace it at no additional cost.

Do you offer white-label link building for agencies?

+
Yes — this is one of our most popular offerings. We deliver fully unbranded reports with your agency's branding if needed. We sign NDAs and provide dedicated Slack channels for agency partners.
