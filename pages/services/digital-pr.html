FAQ

Common Questions

Which publications do you work with?

+
Our network covers 2,000+ publications including Forbes, TechCrunch, BBC, The Guardian, Wired, Business Insider, and thousands of niche trade publications across every industry.

How long does a PR campaign take?

+
First placements typically appear within 4–8 weeks. Data-led campaigns take 6–10 weeks from concept to coverage. Reactive PR can secure placements within days of a news event.

Can you guarantee specific publications?

+
We cannot guarantee specific publications — no ethical agency can. We can target specific tiers and guarantee a minimum number of placements per month based on your plan.

Do placements include backlinks?

+
Most placements include do-follow backlinks. Some top-tier publications (BBC, Forbes) use no-follow links — these still carry enormous brand value and indirect ranking benefit.
